## Recording controls

- Record phone display at native resolution and capture a second external view.
- Disable notifications and personal accounts.
- Use a demo trip with no private home/work coordinates.
- Preserve raw capture, narration, project file, result bundle, and final checksum.
- Verify labels at 100 percent view and on a projector-sized screen.
- Include subtitles and do not use color alone to distinguish modes.
